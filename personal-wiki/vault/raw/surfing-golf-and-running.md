# Surfing, Golf, Running and Everything Else

_Written by me for this wiki, September 2026. First names only for people other than me._

## Golf

I play golf about once a week, usually at Oakland Metro (Metropolitan Golf Links, next to the Oakland airport). My handicap is about a 17.

## Surfing

I surf at Ocean Beach in San Francisco. I've also surfed in Hawaii. Since 2024 I've been a surf volunteer with the MeWater Foundation, which takes kids out on the water.

## Running

I run about 35 miles a week. With the Bear & Axe Run Club at Haas I won a backyard ultra: we ran 50 miles to take the win. I've run 50k-plus distances, and I'm planning my first 100-miler for next year.

## Everything else

I spend a lot of my time with my girlfriend, Tina, who I met this year. When there's time left over, it goes to fantasy football.
